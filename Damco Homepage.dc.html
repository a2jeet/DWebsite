01Introduction

A software company that builds like craftspeople and ships like engineers.

For nearly three decades we've built, modernized, and run custom software for enterprises in 32+ countries—from core insurance platforms to enterprise data pipelines to AI agents running in production.

We're not a strategy shop that hands you a deck, and not a staffing firm that rents you seats. We build the software, tie it to business outcomes, and own those outcomes.

We build AI-native. AI accelerates design, code, and testing while experienced engineers provide architecture, judgement, and accountability.
